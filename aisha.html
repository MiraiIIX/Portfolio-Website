The Brief

They wanted an ad. We built a story.

Aisha makes confiture. Good confiture. They came with a simple ask: a product ad. What we created together was something closer to a short film — one that earns its place in a feed rather than interrupting it.

The client had a clear sense of what they wanted the audience to feel. My job was to translate that feeling into a visual language.

The Process

Every frame was a decision.

01
Brief & Story
We mapped the narrative together — what the product says, what emotion it carries, and what the audience should feel at the end. The story came before a single frame was designed.
02
Voice & Tone
The voiceover wasn't an afterthought. We wrote it as part of the brief — deciding on the pace, the warmth, and what should be said versus what should be felt.
03
3D & Motion
The final piece was brought to life through cinematic 3D animation — giving the product a visual presence that no live shoot could have achieved for the same investment.
The Thinking

Focus is a creative decision.

Aisha knew what they wanted: one video, done right. That kind of clarity is rare, and it's exactly what makes the difference between a good piece of content and a great one. When there's no scope creep, there's nowhere for the quality to hide.

Sometimes the most powerful creative decision is knowing what not to add.

The result is a product ad that doesn't feel like one. It earns your attention for thirty seconds and leaves you with an impression of the brand — not just the product. That's the work.
