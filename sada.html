The Brief

Audio exists. Now make it visible.

Sada is a Moroccan podcast platform built around audio storytelling — long-form content that deserves to live beyond the podcast player. Their challenge: how do you bring audio to social media in a way that actually stops people from scrolling?

They came with the scripts. They came with the audio. They needed someone who could take those raw ingredients and turn them into visual content that matched the depth of what was being said.

The audio tells the story. The visuals make you feel it.

The Approach

Every episode, a different world.

Podcast content lives or dies by how it's packaged for short-form. The challenge isn't just making things look nice — it's finding the visual language that serves the story without competing with it. For each episode, the imagery had to feel like it was born from the words, not placed on top of them.

Working entirely with AI, I created distinct visual environments for each episode — matching tone, pacing, and mood to the audio. The result is a body of work where no two episodes look the same, but all of them feel like Sada.
