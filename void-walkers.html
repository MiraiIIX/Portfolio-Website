The Brief

Launch the jersey. Make it land.

Void Walkers, a rising Moroccan esport team, came with a clear vision: they needed their new jersey to hit. Not just exist — but make noise. The ask was a full creative package: a campaign built to perform on social, and a teaser that would stop the scroll the moment it appeared.

They gave me two product reference images. That was it. The rest was creative direction.

The Approach

Two images. An entire world.

From those two reference images, I built a complete creative universe. No studio. No location scouts. No production crew. Every visual — the lighting setups, the atmospheric compositions, the cinematic color grading — was conceived and executed through AI-driven creative direction.

A full esport jersey campaign, delivered from two photos. Entirely through AI.

The teaser was treated like a proper short film — shot-listed, paced deliberately, and formatted natively for Instagram Reels. The campaign photos were art directed to feel editorial rather than promotional: the kind of imagery that makes you stop and ask where it was shot.
