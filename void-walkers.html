Void Walkers: two photos became a whole world.

A full jersey-release campaign and teaser film for a Moroccan esport team — built entirely with AI from two product reference images.

The brief ⵣ

Launch the jersey. Make it land.

Void Walkers, a rising Moroccan esport team, needed their new jersey to hit — not just exist. The ask was a full creative package: a campaign built to perform on social, and a teaser that stops the scroll the moment it appears.

They gave me two product reference images. That was it. The rest was creative direction.

The approach ⵣ

Two images. An entire world.

From those two references I built a complete creative universe — the lighting setups, the atmospheric compositions, the cinematic color grading — all conceived and executed through AI-driven creative direction. No studio, no location scouts, no production crew.

A full esport jersey campaign, delivered from two photos.

The teaser was treated like a proper short film — shot-listed, paced deliberately, and formatted natively for Reels. The campaign photos were art-directed to feel editorial rather than promotional: imagery that makes you ask where it was shot.
